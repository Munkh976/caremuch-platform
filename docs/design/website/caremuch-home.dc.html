The problem

Most agencies run the rules from memory and spreadsheets.

Scheduling sits in one tool, training forms in a binder, credentials in a spreadsheet, notes on paper and billing in an export. The program lead is the integration layer — and a missed retraining or an expired authorization surfaces months later as a payback.

One rule-aware system

The plan of service drives everything downstream.

Plans, training, credentials, authorizations, shifts, notes and billing share one record. When one changes, the others know — and the schedule refuses what would fail an audit.

Care plans with versions

Build plans from your own templates and goal measures. Revise with a full history; a new version stays pending until staff are trained, while the old one keeps visits running.

Training-gated scheduling

Only caregivers with current credentials and training on the client's current plan appear as eligible. Overrides need a reason and are logged.

Auto-assign, continuity first

Fill a week of open shifts in one pass, favoring the caregivers each client already knows. You preview every proposal and confirm — nothing is saved behind your back. Weights are set per office.

Authorizations that count down

Units draw first-in, first-out across authorizations, with weekly and monthly caps. Units at risk show early; a shift past an authorization or plan end date is refused when it's created.

Visit notes on the phone

Caregivers write against the plan's goals at the end of the visit. Reviewers approve or return with a comment, one deliberate step at a time, with undo.

Billing in true 15-minute units

Choose your office's unit rule — full blocks only, a grace period, or nearest. Weekly billing by authorization or by day, with the rule stored on every note and a clean CSV export.

Audit-ready by default

See what will expire before it costs you.

A compliance dashboard colors every credential, training and authorization by days left. Training sessions, sign-in sheets and files live with the plan they belong to, in private storage, and every change carries who and when.

How a week runs

From plan to paid, without re-keying.

  1. MonPlanA revised plan arrives. Publish it as pending; the system lists who must be retrained.
  2. TueTrainRecord the in-service and attendees. The plan takes effect when everyone on the team is trained.
  3. WedScheduleAuto-assign next week. Unassign, cancel or mark no-shows with a reason that stays on record.
  4. DailyDocumentCaregivers clock the visit and write the note on their phone. Reviewers clear the queue.
  5. FriBillApproved notes become units against the right authorization. Export and submit.
