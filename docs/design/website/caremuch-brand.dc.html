The mark · “Sunrise heart”
CareMuch

A heart that leans 21° to the right, toward the person it cares for. A sunrise-orange glow in the upper-left brings life and warmth; the body is the sky-to-care-blue of the “Much” in the wordmark, and a thin blue rim always closes the edge.

Favicon & app icon

Replaces the Lovable icon in the browser tab. The same heart at every size; the app icon reverses it to white on a blue tile with the sunrise in its corner.
