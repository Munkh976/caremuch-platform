What is Ripple Effects Community Inclusion Center? Ripple Effects Community Inclusion Center is a dedicated space in Portage, Michigan, that champions inclusivity. We offer a range of services, from Social Skills Clubs and Support Groups to Educational Workshops, all tailored to ensure every individual feels valued, understood, and included.

Who can benefit from your services? Anyone and everyone. Our core mission is to bridge gaps and build connections, so whether you're an individual seeking to enhance your interpersonal skills, a family member of someone with special needs, or just someone who champions inclusivity, there's something at Ripple Effects for you.

How do you ensure inclusivity at Ripple Effects? Inclusivity is at the heart of everything we do. From our tailored programs to our dedicated staff and volunteers trained in inclusivity, we ensure that every individual, regardless of their background or abilities, is treated with respect, dignity, and understanding. Our commitment extends beyond just our programs; it's embedded in our ethos and the way we approach every interaction.

Are your programs and events accessible to individuals with disabilities? Absolutely. As an inclusive business, we ensure that all our events and programs are accessible and accommodating to those with disabilities.

How do you ensure the safety of participants in your programs? Safety is a top priority for us. All our programs are overseen by trained professionals, and we have stringent safety and emergency protocols in place. Additionally, our venues are chosen and designed with safety and accessibility in mind.

What is Community Living Supports (CLS)? Community Living Support (CLS) helps individuals with special needs improve their independence and integration into the community. It focuses on personal growth, daily living skills, and social participation, aiming to enhance the quality of life and self-sufficiency of each person.

Do you accept Medicaid? Yes. Many families access our Respite Care and Community Living Supports through Michigan Medicaid, including the Children's Waiver Program and other Home and Community-Based Services. To begin, families typically contact their local Community Mental Health agency and request Ripple Effects as their provider.

How do I get started? The first step is usually contacting your local Community Mental Health agency to begin the eligibility and intake process, then requesting Ripple Effects Community Inclusion Center as your provider. You can also contact us directly or schedule a tour.

Are you a nonprofit? Yes, Ripple Effects Community Inclusion Center is a 501(c)(3) tax-exempt nonprofit organization serving families and individuals in Michigan.
