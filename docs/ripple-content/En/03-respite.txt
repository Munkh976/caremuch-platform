Yes, Ripple Effects provides respite care. Caring for a loved one with unique needs is rewarding, but it can also be overwhelming at times. Our respite care services give caregivers a well-deserved break while ensuring their child or family member is safe, supported, and engaged in meaningful activities.

Respite care provides short-term, temporary relief for primary caregivers. Whether you need time to rest, run errands, or simply recharge, our trained Direct Support Professionals step in to provide compassionate care tailored to your loved one's needs.

We believe respite care should be more than time off for caregivers — it is also an opportunity for growth, inclusion, and joy for those receiving care. Our team focuses on engagement and fun through interactive activities and social opportunities, safety and comfort in a supportive environment, and personalized support that meets the unique needs, interests, and goals of every individual.

Our respite services are available for individuals of all abilities, with a special focus on supporting children, teens, and young adults with developmental differences or special needs. Respite care is also an option for foster parents.

Respite care hours: From September 1st to May 31st, we are open Monday through Thursday 9:00am to 7:00pm, and Fridays 9:00am to 3:00pm. From June 1st to August 31st, we are open Monday through Friday 9:00am to 3:00pm. Saturday and Sunday hours year-round are 9:00am to 4:00pm. For scheduling purposes, we request that caregivers schedule respite care at least one week in advance.

Many families access respite care through Michigan Medicaid programs, which can cover the cost of these services for eligible individuals. Foster parents may also qualify for respite support through Medicaid or foster care services.
