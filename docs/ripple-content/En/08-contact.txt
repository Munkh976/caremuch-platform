Ripple Effects Community Inclusion Center is located at 2255 West Centre Avenue, Portage, Michigan 49024.

You can reach us by phone at 269-257-9060. Our fax number is 269-257-9060. You can email us at info@rippleeffectsinclusion.org.

Our offices are open Monday through Friday from 10:00am to 4:30pm. Respite care is available seven days a week from 8:00am to 8:00pm.

We proudly support families across Kalamazoo County and surrounding southwest Michigan communities, including individuals receiving Medicaid, Community Living Supports, and foster care support.

To get started with inclusive support services, you can contact us, schedule a tour, or fill out the contact form on our website. Our team will be glad to welcome your family and talk through next steps.
