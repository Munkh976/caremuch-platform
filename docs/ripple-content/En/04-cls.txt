Community Living Supports, or CLS, are personalized services designed to help children, teens, and adults with developmental or intellectual disabilities live as independently as possible. Our trained staff provide hands-on support tailored to each individual's unique needs.

CLS includes daily living skills such as meal preparation, personal hygiene, and household tasks; social and community engagement such as building friendships, participating in local activities, and using public transportation; self-advocacy such as learning to make choices, communicate needs, and set personal goals; routine and structure support such as establishing schedules and strategies for independence; and skill-building through goal-oriented support to increase confidence and self-sufficiency.

Many families access CLS through Michigan Medicaid programs, which can cover the cost of these services for eligible individuals. CLS is typically available for individuals who qualify for Medicaid Waiver programs, including the Developmental Disabilities (DD) Waiver or MiABLE/HCBS services.

To get started with CLS, families can apply for Medicaid Waiver services online or through the Michigan Department of Health and Human Services (MDHHS). Your local MDHHS office can guide you through the process. Once approved, a care coordinator will help determine which services and hours you qualify for, and our team will partner with you to schedule CLS sessions, create a personalized plan, and begin providing support.
