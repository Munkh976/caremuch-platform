Ripple Effects Community Inclusion Center provides two kinds of support: therapeutic care services and inclusive community programs.

Therapeutic care services include Respite Care, Community Living Supports (CLS), and Music Therapy. These are delivered by our trained Direct Support Professionals and are often funded through Michigan Medicaid.

Inclusive community programs include social skills clubs, day camps and summer day camps, adult programs and events, a Community Learning Hub (with independent studies and homeschool family connections), and cultural and wellness classes. Our classes include Seasonal Cooking, Taiji and Ba Duan Jin wellness, Mandarin and Mahjong, and Chinese Tea Culture. We also offer clubs such as the Sensory Seekers Club for various age groups.

Additional community offerings include the Waves Sensory Hair Salon and a Community Closet.

Every program is designed to build confidence, connection, and independence for children, teens, and adults of all abilities. Our programs are relationship-centered and designed to support individuals with disabilities and their families through social skills groups, respite care, and community-based programs.
