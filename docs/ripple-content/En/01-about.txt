Ripple Effects Community Inclusion Center is a community haven located in Portage, Michigan, serving Kalamazoo County and surrounding southwest Michigan communities. We are a 501(c)(3) tax-exempt nonprofit organization serving families and individuals in Michigan. Our tagline is "Connect. Grow. Thrive Together," and our belief is simple: everyone belongs.

We offer inclusive recreational, educational, and social clubs alongside therapeutic care services. No matter your unique journey, our community center is designed to help you connect, grow, and thrive — a space where potential meets opportunity, open to everyone.

Our mission is to offer individualized, evidence-based support and enrichment for individuals and their families, fostering an inclusive environment where everyone can thrive socially, academically, and recreationally. Our vision is to establish a community-driven center where every person, regardless of their abilities or challenges, feels valued, understood, and empowered to achieve their fullest potential.

We proudly support families across Kalamazoo County and surrounding communities, including individuals receiving Medicaid, Community Living Supports, and foster care support. Ripple Effects began with our Autism Learning Centers in 2020 and has grown to serve families across Central and Southwest Michigan.
